About

For more than 13 years I have worked where impurity specifications meet production reality: wet chemicals, CMP slurries, CVD/ALD precursors, specialty gases and wafers.

When a lot goes out of spec, I am usually the one finding out why. That work runs from method development and validation through root-cause analysis to laboratory management, and it has taken me on site to fab customers in Korea and China.

A good analyst should be able to explain every number they sign. That is why I write about the fundamentals behind a certificate of analysis, and why I have started using AI to take some of the reporting work off the analyst’s desk.
